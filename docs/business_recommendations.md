# Business Recommendations — K-Means Customer Segmentation

## 1. Overview

The final K-Means model identified three customer segments based on
purchasing behavior.

The recommended business strategies are designed around the observed
differences in recency, frequency, monetary value, average order
value, and product variety.

The recommendations are strategic interpretations of the clustering
results and should be validated against actual business objectives
and campaign performance before deployment.

---

## 2. Recommendation 1 — Re-engage At-Risk / Low-Value Customers

### Target Segment

**Cluster 0 — At-Risk / Low-Value Customers**

- 1,221 customers
- 28.15% of the customer base
- Average recency: 163.83 days
- Average frequency: 1.39
- Average monetary value: 215.00

### Recommended Action

Develop targeted re-engagement campaigns for customers in this
segment.

Possible approaches include:

- Personalized product recommendations
- Limited-time promotional offers
- Reminder campaigns
- New-product announcements
- Incentives designed to encourage another purchase

### Objective

Increase purchase recency and encourage inactive or low-engagement
customers to return.

---

## 3. Recommendation 2 — Retain and Reward High-Value Customers

### Target Segment

**Cluster 1 — High-Value Loyal Customers**

- 1,188 customers
- 27.39% of the customer base
- Average recency: 25.13 days
- Average frequency: 10.28
- Average monetary value: 5,918.11
- Average order value: 679.84

### Recommended Action

Prioritize retention and relationship-building strategies for this
segment.

Possible approaches include:

- Loyalty rewards
- Early access to new products
- Personalized recommendations
- Premium customer experiences
- Targeted cross-selling and upselling

### Objective

Protect the highest-value customer relationship and encourage
continued purchasing activity.

---

## 4. Recommendation 3 — Convert Regular Customers into High-Value Customers

### Target Segment

**Cluster 2 — Regular / Growth Customers**

- 1,929 customers
- 44.47% of the customer base
- Largest identified segment
- Average frequency: 2.40
- Average monetary value: 826.33

### Recommended Action

Use targeted growth campaigns to increase purchase frequency and
customer value.

Possible approaches include:

- Product recommendations based on previous purchases
- Cross-selling related products
- Bundled offers
- Personalized promotions
- Campaigns encouraging repeat purchases

### Objective

Move suitable customers from the regular/growth segment toward the
higher-engagement characteristics observed in Cluster 1.

---

## 5. Recommended Business Priority

Based on the observed cluster characteristics, a practical priority
order is:

1. **Retain Cluster 1** because it represents the strongest
   customer-value segment.
2. **Grow Cluster 2** because it is the largest segment and provides
   the largest population for potential development.
3. **Re-engage Cluster 0** because it shows the weakest recent and
   historical purchasing activity.

---

## 6. Important Limitation

The recommendations are derived from clustering patterns and should
not be interpreted as proven causal effects.

Before implementing campaigns, the business should test different
strategies and measure outcomes such as:

- Repeat purchase rate
- Customer retention
- Revenue per customer
- Campaign conversion rate
- Average order value
- Customer response rate

This would allow the organization to determine whether the proposed
segment-specific strategies produce measurable improvements.
